1. Direct Exam Eligibility: Students with a minimum overall attendance of 75% or higher are directly eligible to sit for semester examinations.
2. Condonation Eligibility Range: Attendance between 65% and 74.99% falls under the condonation range. Students in this range may apply for condonation of shortage.
3. Condonation Requirements: Condonation of attendance shortage (65% to 74.99%) requires submitting a valid medical certificate and paying a mandatory condonation fee of $50.
4. Detention Policy: Students with attendance below 65% are strictly detained from appearing in examinations and must re-register for the course in a subsequent semester.
5. Medical Leave Counting: Approved medical leave (up to 10 days) permits condonation eligibility but does not directly count as attended classes in the percentage calculation.
6. Duty Leave (Sports/NCC/Events): Approved duty leave for representing the institution in official sports, NCC, or cultural events (up to 15 days) is treated as present and added directly to classes attended.
7. Medical Certificate Submission Deadline: Medical certificates must be submitted to the Academic Office within 7 calendar days of returning to classes, or the request will be rejected.
8. Course-Level Minimum Attendance: In addition to the aggregate attendance, a minimum of 70% attendance is required in each individual registered subject/course.
9. Exceptional Dean's Condonation: Condonation below 65% attendance is prohibited except under extraordinary approval granted by the Dean of Academic Affairs for national-level event representation.
